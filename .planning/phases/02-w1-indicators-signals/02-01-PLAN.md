<objective>
Refactor `MarketPulseService` so market breadth aggregation can run on any `Timeframe`, not just D1. The existing `computeDaily()` method hardcodes `Timeframe.D1`; this is the single hardcoded timeframe in the entire indicator/signal/pulse pipeline. Extract a new public `computeForTimeframe(Timeframe)` method and make `computeDaily()` delegate to it.

Purpose: SGNL-02 (W1 market breadth) cannot be satisfied without a timeframe-parameterized entry point. This refactor is a prerequisite for the W1 orchestrator in Plan 02.
Output: `MarketPulseService` with a `computeForTimeframe(Timeframe)` public method and a backward-compatible `computeDaily()`.
</objective>
